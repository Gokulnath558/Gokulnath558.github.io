Maharashtra



  • Western Ghats (Sahyadri): Forms a range parallel to the coast, with slopes descending eastward.
  • Plateau: A plateau of plateaus, with ranges like Satpura, Ajanta, and Balaghat.
  • Konkan Coast: Features lush landscapes and beautiful beaches.
  • Biogeographic Zones: Includes Western Ghats, Deccan Plateau, and West Coast, hosting unique flora and fauna.
  • Sub-regional Cultures: Influences from Vidarbha (Central Provinces) and Konkan create diverse local identities.
  • Historical Sites: Home to UNESCO World Heritage Sites like Ajanta & Ellora Caves, Elephanta Caves, and Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus.
